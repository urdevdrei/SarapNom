Our Sweet Story

SarapNom is a local bakery that sells sweet delights which started in 2025. We are famous for our home-made Dubai Chewy Cookie and many other products. SarapNom aims to provide affordable yet quality products for the consumers to make them want to go back again and again.

Vision & Mission

Our guiding principles that shape every dessert we create and every smile we deliver

Our Vision

To become the go-to food discovery platform in the Philippines, connecting people with authentic dining experiences and local food communities.

Our Mission

We help users easily discover great restaurants, cafés, and food spots while supporting local food businesses by giving them greater online visibility. We also aim to build a trusted community where people can share honest reviews and recommendations, and we celebrate Filipino food culture while embracing new dining trends.
